ABOUT US

Reliable Indonesian Coconut Supply for Global Buyers.

```

CV Nurio Jaya Sinergi is an Indonesian supplier and exporter of mature coconut and coconut-derived products for international buyers, manufacturers, distributors and trading partners.

We supply Indonesian coconut raw materials and coconut-based products for food, horticulture, agriculture, biomass and industrial applications.

Our product range includes mature coconut, cocopeat, cocofiber, coconut shell charcoal and raw coconut shell, depending on availability and buyer requirements.

For every export inquiry, we can discuss product specifications, quantity, packaging, shipment requirements and commercial terms according to the buyer's needs.
```
